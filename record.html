Press the record button and allow the microphone.
How to record
Hold Space (or hold the red button) while you speak, and let go when you finish. It jumps to the next line by itself. P plays your take back, ←/→ move between lines, and holding again re-records a line.

For the best sound: use a quiet room with soft things around you (a closet full of clothes is great), stay a hand's width from the mic, and start speaking right after you press. Your takes are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back later.

When you're done: click Download my recordings and send the .zip to the person making the game.
